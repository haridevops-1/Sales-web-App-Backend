Zoho CRM & Marketing Automation for iSteel

Prepared for iSteel (VIPL), a TMT steel manufacturer working through a large dealer network and an extensive influencer ecosystem. A two-phase Zoho CRM + Campaigns engagement — structuring how iSteel communicates with leads, influencers and dealers, and how Technical Engineers map sites, classify demand and convert it through the dealer network — with Zoho as the engagement layer above SAP, the dealer platform and iSTAR.

◆What we deliver

A configured Zoho CRM + Zoho Campaigns engagement layer above SAP, the dealer platform and iSTAR — structured communication journeys plus a Technical-Engineer-led site-mapping and sales-conversion workflow.

◆The Spikra way

BRD-aligned. Every assumption is made explicit and every open item flagged for the discovery workshop, so scope is confirmed before detailed design is locked.

◆How we support you

Per-system integration decisions, a field pilot for the mobile geotagged capture, role-based training for TEs and SOs, and dedicated Hypercare through go-live.

What iSteel gets from the move
  • Structured, lifecycle-tailored communication journeys for leads, influencers and dealers over WhatsApp, with SMS fallback
  • Influencer self-registration via the iSTAR app (link or QR) — no Technical Engineer visit required to onboard
  • One influencer mapped once, with every project site recorded and tracked individually
  • Technical Engineers classify each site Hot / Warm / Cold; qualified sites auto-route to the Sales Officer by geography
  • Mandatory loss capture — building a usable competitor and objection database
  • Clubs and tiers (Platinum / Gold / Silver) recomputed from rolling won-site tonnage, with full audit history
  • A Campaign Manager for planned, calendar-driven campaigns independent of lifecycle triggers
  • Role-based visibility and field-level audit history on Lead Type, stage, owner and requirement quantity
